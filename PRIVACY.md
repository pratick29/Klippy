# Privacy Policy for Klippy

**Last Updated: August 4, 2026**

**Klippy** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the Klippy Chrome Extension.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Storage Location

Klippy operates under a strict **Zero Data Collection** model:
- **Local Storage Only:** All data saved in Klippy (profile links, passwords, API keys, and secure notes) is stored strictly on your local device using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **No External Servers:** We do not operate external servers, databases, or analytics services. Your data never leaves your browser.
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** Klippy does not include analytics, telemetry, ad trackers, or third-party SDKs.

---

## 2. Chrome Extension Permissions

Klippy requests the minimum permissions necessary to provide its core functionality:

- `storage`: Required to save your encrypted vault data, preferences, and custom links locally on your device.
- `tabs`: Required exclusively to detect when you are on supported profile pages (e.g., GitHub, HackerRank) so Klippy can show the optional auto-save banner, and to open links (such as your portfolio or Ko-fi support page) in a new tab when clicked.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Security

- **Master Password Protection:** Passwords and keys saved in Klippy can be locked using a Master Password.
- **Client-Side Only:** Decryption and password verification happen entirely on your device in your browser memory. We never have access to your Master Password or stored credentials.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

Klippy provides optional external links (such as supporting the creator on Ko-fi or visiting Pratick Labs). Clicking these links opens external websites subject to their respective privacy policies. Klippy itself does not pass any user data to these third parties.

---

## 5. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published in our public GitHub repository.
